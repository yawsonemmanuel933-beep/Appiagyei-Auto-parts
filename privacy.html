Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SYNC-STORE ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your data.

Data We Collect

The app stores the following data locally on your device and optionally syncs it to a private server you control:

  • Transaction data — Sales records, items sold, prices, payment methods
  • Inventory data — Brand, car type, year, side/part, prices, quantities
  • Customer names — Customer names and optional contact details (email, phone)
  • Supplier information — Supplier names and contact details
  • User accounts — Usernames and hashed passwords for system access
  • Audit logs — Records of actions taken within the system

How We Use Data

All data is used solely for the purpose of operating the point-of-sale system:

  • Processing sales transactions
  • Managing inventory and stock levels
  • Generating sales reports and analytics
  • Managing customer and supplier relationships
  • Providing role-based access control

Data Storage & Security

  • Local storage: Data is stored in your browser's localStorage and an on-device SQLite database
  • Server storage: When syncing is enabled, data is transmitted via HTTPS to your private server and stored in a SQLite database
  • Passwords: User passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage. Plain-text passwords are never stored
  • Transmission: All data sent between the app and your server is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third parties. All data remains on your devices and your private server.

Data Retention

Data is retained until you choose to delete it. You can clear all records from the app's settings at any time. Database files can be deleted manually from the server.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can view, modify, or delete any records through the app interface
  • You can clear all data from the dashboard
  • You can stop syncing and keep data only locally
  • You can delete the server database file at any time

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the system administrator of your instance.
